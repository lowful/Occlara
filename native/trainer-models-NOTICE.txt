The Trainer's models (src/renderer/trainer/models/guns.js and figure.js)

Every model the Trainer shows is made from the seven sources below by
scripts/trainer-models/build.js. Each source's own page on poly.pizza was read
on 19 Nov 2026 and states its licence as Public Domain (CC0). CC0 asks for no
credit; the authors are named here because they did the work. The downloads
themselves are not in this repository: scripts/trainer-models/fetch.js gets
them again from the addresses in scripts/trainer-models/sources.json.

Nothing here is taken from, named after or made to look like anything of
VALORANT's or any other game's: no agent, no weapon, no knife, no glove. The
sources are generic low poly models of real world kit, each published by the
person who made it.

1. SWAT, by Quaternius
   https://poly.pizza/m/Btfn3G5Xv4
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the bots' figure (mesh, skeleton, and its Idle_Neutral, Run,
   Run_Back, Run_Left, Run_Right and Death clips), and the forearms and
   gloved hands of the five first person view models.
   Changed: recoloured (dark fatigues, black kit and gloves; the bare skin of
   the fingers and round the eyes painted as glove and mask, so no skin
   shows); normals smoothed by angle; scaled to 95% so the helmet stands at
   the head hitbox's height; turned to face -z. The upper body of every clip
   is posed again by the build to aim a rifle (spine, head, both arms reached
   onto the rifle, upper arms lengthened by 30%); the standing and crouched
   stances are built from the level stance with the hips lowered and the
   feet moved; the fingers are curled once and baked into the mesh, so the
   skeleton that ships has 22 bones. Each clip is sampled to a few frames
   (10, 6, 16 a run, 14 for the fall). For the view models the forearm and
   hand are cut from the figure, posed onto each weapon, scaled to 80% and
   the sleeve stretched so its cut end is out of the frame.

2. Rifle West, by Pichuliru
   https://poly.pizza/m/wED1MSx2SK
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the Heavy rifle's view model.
   Changed: recoloured, each material given a gloss (polymer or metal);
   normals smoothed by angle; hands added.

3. Assault Rifle West, by Pichuliru
   https://poly.pizza/m/ss1nz3gJnx
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the Silenced rifle's view model (with source 4 on its muzzle),
   and, at 90% of its size and with no suppressor, the rifle the bots hold.
   Changed: recoloured, glossed, normals smoothed by angle; hands added.

4. Suppressor, by Pichuliru
   https://poly.pizza/m/QWfBIqy0VW
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the Silenced rifle's suppressor, set on the rifle's own muzzle
   attachment point.
   Changed: recoloured, glossed, normals smoothed by angle.

5. Pistol West, by Pichuliru
   https://poly.pizza/m/r8RjoAwN5A
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the Light pistol's view model.
   Changed: recoloured, glossed, normals smoothed by angle; hand added.

6. Kabar, by Pichuliru
   https://poly.pizza/m/bAaO335d6A
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the knife's view model.
   Changed: recoloured (the grip dark), glossed, normals smoothed by angle;
   turned to stand in a fist, blade up and ahead; hand added.

7. Revolver, by Quaternius
   https://poly.pizza/m/9C26wSpMS0
   Licence: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)
   Used for: the Heavy pistol's view model.
   Changed: recoloured (dark grip, steel frame, bright cylinder), glossed,
   normals smoothed by angle; scaled to 0.32 m long and turned to point down
   -z; hand added.

CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No texture ships: every colour is a vertex colour set by the build. No loader
ships either: the meshes are packed into plain JavaScript modules that
src/renderer/trainer/kit.js unpacks, so three.js stays the one vendored file
(native/three-NOTICE.txt).
