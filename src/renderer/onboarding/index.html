You're in.

Four quick things, then you're playing.

  1. 1
    Press Start before your matchSet your game to Windowed Fullscreen, then hit the red button on the panel. Nothing appears on your screen while you play.
  2. 2
    Confirm your agentA bubble on the panel asks who you're playing. Confirm it, or tap one of your mains, so the review can talk about your abilities.
  3. 3
    Read your graded reviewWhen the match ends it opens on its own: a grade out of 100, your repeated mistakes, what went well, and what you missed.

Keep it out of your way

The panel should never catch your mouse mid-aim.

While you play, minimize the panel with Ctrl+Shift+M. Occlara keeps reading your game in the background, and the same shortcut brings the panel back.

Last reviewCtrl+Shift+E
MatchesCtrl+Shift+H
Minimize panelCtrl+Shift+M
Pause / resumeCtrl+Shift+P

During a match Ctrl+Shift+E does nothing, on purpose: Occlara shows you nothing while you play, and everything it read is waiting in the review when the match ends.

Every review is kept under Matches, where Occlara also tracks what keeps repeating across your games.

One last thing

Your Riot ID makes the grade exact.

Riot's record of each match checks every death Occlara read off your screen, adds who killed you and when, and counts your trades and clutches. Use the account you play on.

Advanced coaching lets your reviews draw on damage breakpoints, utility timings and reads across rounds. Newer players usually leave it off.
