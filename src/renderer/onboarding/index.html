Keep it out of your way

Occlara sits behind your game like any other app.

While you play, Ctrl+Shift+M hides the window. Occlara keeps reading your game in the background, and the same shortcut brings it back.

Last reviewCtrl+Shift+E
MatchesCtrl+Shift+H

During a match Ctrl+Shift+E does nothing, on purpose: Occlara shows you nothing while you play, and everything it read is waiting in the review when the match ends.
