Settings

Capture speed

Faster reads catch more of every fight. Auto keeps up by itself.

How a round is read

How often Occlara reads your screen in a live round. Menus, buy phases and the time spent dead are read less often. Faster reads catch more of every fight, so the review has more to go on. Auto starts at one read a second and keeps it while the AI keeps up, even when a reply takes a few seconds, and steps down on its own only if replies lag further or keep failing. The capture runs in its own process, outside the game.

Record match video

Recorded on this PC, so the review can show a clip of each moment. The video never leaves this PC.

What On and Off do

Clips

Clips kept beside your reviews

Space the clips may take together. Past it, the oldest reviews lose their clips first and keep their frames and facts.

Riot ID

Name#TAG of the account you play Valorant on. A full grade needs it.

What Riot's record adds

Riot's record of each match checks every death the screen read, adds who killed you and when, and counts trades and clutches.

Advanced coaching

Lets your review draw on the deeper material: damage breakpoints, utility timings and reads across rounds.

What stays in either way

Fundamentals always stay in, and they take over when a match shows you dying on repeat.

Performance

Smaller frames for an older PC or a slow connection. The reads get less reliable.

What is read at each size

Screenshots are read at 720p so health, the round timer, the score and the location label are legible. Performance sends smaller frames for an older PC or a slow connection, and the reads get less reliable.

Sounds

A short sound when recording starts and another when it stops, so you know it is on without looking away from the game.
