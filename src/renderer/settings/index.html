Settings

Occlara reads your game while you play and shows nothing until the match ends. Then it opens a graded review: your repeated mistakes, what went well, and what you missed. Every review is kept under Matches.

Capture speed

How often Occlara reads your screen during a match. Faster reads catch more of every fight, so the review has more to go on. Auto picks the fastest the connection and the AI can keep up with, and steps down on its own if replies start to lag. No effect on game FPS.

Riot ID

Name#TAG of the account you play Valorant on. A full grade needs it: Riot's record of each match checks every death the screen read, adds who killed you and when, and counts trades and clutches.

Advanced coaching

Lets your review draw on the deeper material: damage breakpoints, utility timings and reads across rounds, built from VOD reviews by Bonkar, head coach of NRG. Fundamentals always stay in, and they take over when a match shows you dying on repeat.

Performance

Screenshots are read at 720p so health, the round timer, the score and the location label are legible. Performance sends smaller frames for an older PC or a slow connection, and the reads get less reliable.

Sounds

A short sound when recording starts and another when it stops, so you know it is on without looking away from the game.

AI log

Keeps the frames Occlara reads and what it parsed from each, for your last few sessions. The review goes back to these to look at the moment before a death, so with this off the review has no frames to show. Sealed while a match is in progress.
